About

Chhaya Bajaj

Chhaya Bajaj (Chhaya Enterprises) has grown from a local sub-dealership to one of the most respected and trusted names in the automotive industry in Varanasi. We believe that buying a vehicle is not just a transaction; it's the start of a lifelong journey with our family.

Our commitment to excellence is reflected in every aspect of our business — from the carefully curated range of Bajaj motorcycles and Chetak electric vehicles in our Bhopapur showroom to our state-of-the-art service center equipped with advanced diagnostic tools.

Whether you are looking for high-octane performance with Pulsar and Dominar, unmatched daily comfort with Platina and Freedom 125, or zero-emission electric mobility with Chetak, Chhaya Bajaj is here to serve you with excellence.
